02 / Inside Nasua

Małe kroki.
Własne rozwiązania.

Projekt łączy klasyczne zagadnienia tworzenia systemów operacyjnych z własnymi pomysłami na architekturę i interfejs.

⌘

Custom kernel

Freestanding C++20 and the development of low-level system components.

▤

ClawFS

A custom filesystem designed specifically for NasuaOS.

▦

Graphical environment

Windows, a taskbar and an interface inspired by classic desktop systems.

⌁

Architectures

Focused primarily on x86_64, with other architectures explored experimentally.

▧

Custom applications

The NAPP format and the foundations of an independent application ecosystem.

⚙

Drivers

Expanding support for hardware, graphics and input/output devices.
